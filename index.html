// 01. OVERVIEW & PROTOCOL MESHTASTIC • LORA 915 MHZ
Autonomous Mesh Architecture

Decentralized, open-source Meshtastic communications for the Pend Oreille basin.

PRMesh is an open-source, community-driven radio mesh network built exclusively on the Meshtastic® protocol. Operating across the Lake Pend Oreille basin, the Pend Oreille River corridor, and adjacent mountain valleys, it provides reliable off-grid text messaging, location sharing, and emergency coordination without relying on commercial cellular towers, internet service providers, or municipal power grids.

Meshtastic Standard

100% compatible with official Meshtastic software and standard iOS and Android apps connected via Bluetooth.

License-Free 915 MHz

Operates under FCC Part 15 regulations in the 902–928 MHz ISM band. Anyone can participate freely without licensing exams.

Disaster Resilience

Engineered for emergency community messaging during severe winter storms, blizzards, sub-zero cold, and prolonged power outages.

How Meshtastic Works: Every Meshtastic device acts simultaneously as a private communicator and an autonomous relay node. Packets hop dynamically from radio to radio across valleys, lake surfaces, and ridges—creating a self-healing local infrastructure that grows stronger with every active node.
